HOW TO PLAY SUS WORD

3 min a round
1

Join

Type !join, open the card link, enter your Twitch name, send !join CODE.

2

Play your role

Your card shows it. The Imposter hides a secret word in a normal sentence. Detectives chat and hunt for it.

3

Vote

When the timer ends, type your suspect's number in chat or tap them on your card. New roles every round.

⚠ The name on your card must be your exact Twitch name.

Example: the Imposter's word is LEMON. They say “I take my tea with lemon and honey”. Spot it and vote for them.

Pro move: !accuse name word
